Projects

Quantum + ML

Hybrid Quantum-Classical ML for IC50 Prediction

Problem

Can hybrid quantum-classical regressors predict drug response (IC50) as accurately as classical models, and at what computational cost?

Data

GDSC (13,106 samples) and Anchor Combination (130,776 samples).

Method

Three quantum encodings (angle, amplitude, hybrid) simulated in PennyLane and compared with classical regressors, with COBYLA proxy optimization and Differential Evolution.

Results

R² = 0.8636 for a hybrid Random Forest on a 5,000-sample Anchor Combination proxy (ΔR² = 0.025 vs. the classical baseline, R² = 0.8882); amplitude-encoded hybrid MLP used up to 75% less memory.

Quantum Hardware

Quantum Encoding Benchmark on a 20-Qubit QPU

Problem

How do quantum data-encoding strategies behave for IC50 prediction on real quantum hardware?

Data

GDSC2.

Method

12 encoding strategies run on the IQM Garnet 20-qubit superconducting QPU using Qrisp.

Results

First hardware-executed benchmark of twelve encodings; a QAOA-inspired encoding performed best. Published as a bioRxiv preprint with code, notebooks and results.
